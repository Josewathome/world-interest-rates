Data from free public sources: Federal Reserve (FRED), ECB, Bank of England, Bank of Canada, Central Bank of Brazil, and the Bank for International Settlements (BIS) policy-rate database. Inflation (CPI) is shown for US/Euro Area/Brazil only, labeled with its actual reference month — CPI always lags by weeks to months everywhere, unlike a rate decision. FX rates are ECB daily reference rates (frankfurter.dev); a few currencies aren't covered. US rate expectations are market-implied estimates we calculate ourselves from CME Fed Funds futures pricing (via an unofficial data feed) — not an official Fed forecast, and flagged when the underlying price looks stale.
